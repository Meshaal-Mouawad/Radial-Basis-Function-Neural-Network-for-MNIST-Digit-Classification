## Author statements and independently inferred gap

The distinction is explicit in every ledger record. No author is attributed a statement that “uniform nearest-prototype agreement over bandwidth and ridge remains open.”

Author statements actually inspected include Saralajew’s discussion of deep-model interpretability and expert interpretation of components, PALACE’s restriction to its nearest-centroid certificate and its reported certificate firing, and Que’s computational burden for full RBF models. These are those authors’ stated boundaries.

The proposed R5 gap is **independently inferred** from the mathematical objects and demonstrated results: prescribed potential surfaces, statistical approximation, input-robust constrained readout, fixed-design penalty paths, and representation learning collectively leave the exact R5 parameter-region property untreated in the evidence inspected. This inference does not require those authors to have declared the question.

The gap survives as a bounded, source-grounded residual question.
