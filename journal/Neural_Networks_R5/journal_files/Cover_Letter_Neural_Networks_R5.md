# Transfer manuscript: Neural Networks

2026-11-15 | Draft cover letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider Training-Derived Regions of Nearest-Prototype Agreement in Gaussian RBF Classifiers as a research article. The manuscript was previously submitted to Neurocomputing under NEUCOM-D-26-25836, and I selected Neural Networks for the proposed transfer.

A nearest prototype offers a simple label summary for a radial basis function classifier. The learned output weights can produce a different class, creating a question about when that summary remains valid as bandwidth and regularization change. The manuscript develops training-derived tests that certify agreement across continuous parameter regions, identify strict exclusions with finite disagreement thresholds, and certify local bandwidth bands.

The two-dataset evaluation covers five seeds, three prototype budgets and 2,880 archived readouts. It establishes the measured reach of the guarantees: every validation-competitive readout under the primary rule has zero full-width sufficient coverage, finite penalty paths exhibit returns to agreement, and class-conditioned comparisons explain accuracy within certified subsets. The revised literature synthesis connects this question to finite-scale identity of nearest-neighbour and exponential-potential rules and to regularization-path guarantees.

The complete supplement preserves the training evidence, protocols, per-query records, reproducible analyses and independent numerical verification. The manuscript contains the original mathematical results, seven figures and twelve tables.

Thank you for considering the work.
