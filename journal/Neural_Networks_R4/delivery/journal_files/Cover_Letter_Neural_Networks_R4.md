# Transfer manuscript: Neural Networks

14 November 2026 | Draft cover letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider Training-Derived Regions of Nearest-Prototype Agreement in Gaussian RBF Classifiers as a research article. The manuscript was previously submitted to Neurocomputing under NEUCOM-D-26-25836; I selected Neural Networks for the proposed transfer.

The work develops training-derived conditions for agreement with a nearest-prototype label throughout Gaussian ridge RBF parameter regions. Its mathematical results certify continuous bandwidth–penalty regions, identify strict large-penalty exclusions with finite disagreement bounds, and certify local bandwidth bands with penalty tails.

This integrated revision exhaustively examines the original 30 dataset–seed–budget groups. It quantifies zero full-width coverage among validation-competitive readouts, nonmonotone agreement along finite regularization paths, class composition within matched selected subsets, and the accumulation of finite exclusions relative to analytical witnesses. These results establish the measured reach of continuous decision-persistence guarantees in the studied model families.

The original evidence, proofs and six figures are preserved. The manuscript adds a class-specific figure and four new tables to the preceding integrated version. The complete supplement retains protocols, per-query arrays, reproducible scripts, hashes, and independent numerical reconstruction.

Thank you for considering the work.
