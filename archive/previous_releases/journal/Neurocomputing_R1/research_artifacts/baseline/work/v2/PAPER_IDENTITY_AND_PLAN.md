## Argument under investigation

Prototype labels offer a simple decision summary for a Gaussian RBF classifier. An unconstrained output readout can choose another class. Recent work in Neurocomputing studies learned-prototype influence and Gaussian scale geometry, while prior RBF work explicitly recognizes the interpretability effect of unconstrained weights. The present research asks whether training data can establish a whole region of bandwidth and ridge-penalty choices in which the nearest-prototype label is guaranteed to match the classifier.

The candidate contribution is a computable sufficient region (0,t] × [lambda0,infinity), derived before readout fitting. A symmetric prototype block permits a class-wise residual decomposition; a lower eigenvalue bound and a two-endpoint penalty envelope yield the regional guarantee. Norm inequalities and eigenvalue tools are established mathematics and will be attributed/described accordingly. Label agreement is the explicit target. The literature comparison must determine the new capability relative to existing results.
